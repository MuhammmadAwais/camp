## Brand & Style

This design system draws inspiration from warm autumn editorial aesthetics, blending tactile warmth with contemporary editorial restraint. It evokes comfort, crafted quality, timeless nostalgia, and quiet sophistication—reminiscent of curated seasonal lookbooks, boutique culinary experiences, and lifestyle journals.

The visual direction merges **Minimalism** with **Tactile Warmth**. It eschews sterile tech monochromes in favor of deep wine undertones, toasted honey, and creamy parchment whites. Layouts rely on ample breathing room, organic silhouettes, and confident typographic hierarchy that feels deliberate and literary.

## Colors

The palette is rooted directly in the seasonal reference study:
- **Primary (`#8C383E`)**: Deep terracotta / burgundy wine (Pantone 2350 U). Used for authoritative brand presence, primary interactive controls, hero headers, and anchor elements.
- **Secondary (`#E59344`)**: Warm honey amber (Pantone P 14-8 U). Serves as an expressive accent, active indicator, highlight tag, and high-energy touchpoint.
- **Tertiary (`#DDA77B`)**: Muted toasted almond. Provides intermediate contrast for secondary callouts, soft badges, and decorative borders.
- **Neutral Base (`#F4E8D7`)**: Oat / cream ivory (Pantone P 24-9 U). Sets a soft, organic reading canvas that eliminates the harsh glare of pure `#FFFFFF`.

### Semantic Applications
- **Surface Canvas**: `#FDF9F3` for highest light tier, `#F4E8D7` for standard page backgrounds, and `#EADDCB` for layered cards or subtle section shifts.
- **Text & Content**: `#2A1617` (espresso plum) for primary high-contrast reading text; `#5E3D3F` for secondary descriptive labels; `#8C383E` for interactive or standalone accent text.
- **Feedback States**: Success maps to earthy sage (`#4D6846`), Warning aligns naturally with the secondary amber (`#E59344`), and Error uses a vibrant crimson-berry (`#A8202A`).

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the structural, characterful shapes of **Epilogue** for display headers with the balanced, warm clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces and body copy. 

- **Display & Headlines (Epilogue)**: Imparts a curated, editorial presence. Tight letter spacing on large display scales produces compact, magazine-like impact.
- **Interface & Text (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: The open counters and friendly geometry keep body text readable and human over warm ivory backgrounds without eye fatigue.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Employs uppercase or tracked semi-bold variations for tags, metadata, and buttons to reinforce deliberate, high-end structure.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a flexible 12-column responsive grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single or 2-column flow on mobile viewports.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column layout with 48px outer margins and 24px column gutters. Section breaks utilize `space-xl` (40px) or multiples thereof to promote relaxed, unhurried pacing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 32px margins and 20px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid structure with 20px outer margin and 16px gutters.

Inner component padding preserves breathing room: inputs, buttons, and cards maintain generous horizontal cushions to reinforce the tactile, uncompressed design tone.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system relies primarily on **Tonal Layers** supplemented by **Warm Ambient Shadows**:

- **Tonal Stepping**: Elevation is communicated through shifts in warmth and brightness rather than high-contrast drops. The background rests at base cream (`#F4E8D7`), elevated cards sit on bleached oat (`#FDF9F3`), and pressed or sunken surfaces step to warm tan (`#EADDCB`).
- **Warm Ambient Shadows**: Where depth requires physical separation (e.g., dropdowns, modals, floating action bars), shadows use a tinted burgundy-umber tone (`rgba(56, 20, 24, 0.08)`) with a broad blur radius (16px to 32px) instead of cold, desaturated black.
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines**: Cards and interactive elements feature a subtle 1px border colored with 10% opacity terracotta (`rgba(140, 56, 62, 0.12)`), establishing boundaries without visual noise.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Rounded** philosophy (`2`), delivering gentle, organic contours that reflect fallen autumn leaves and crafted lifestyle goods:
- Standard interactive elements (buttons, inputs, select fields) have a corner radius of `0.5rem` (8px).
- Larger containers, cards, and modal dialogs adopt `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px) for a soft, welcoming posture.
- Accent badges and pill tags may use full capsules to mirror botanical and organic shapes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid terracotta wine background (`#8C383E`), crisp ivory text (`#F4E8D7`), `0.5rem` radius, semi-bold `label-lg`. On hover, color transitions to a deeper plum-wine (`#752B30`).
- **Secondary**: Clear background with a 1.5px solid terracotta border (`#8C383E`) and matching wine text. On hover, fills with a 6% wash of terracotta.
- **Accent (Seasonal)**: Golden amber fill (`#E59344`) with dark plum text (`#2A1617`) for prominent call-to-actions and seasonal conversion moments.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact capsules (`rounded-full`) with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding. 
- Backgrounds leverage tinted tertiary tones (`rgba(229, 147, 68, 0.15)`) with amber text (`#995412`), or subtle cream-wine duotones.

### Input Fields
- Filled container style with `#FDF9F3` surface, framed by a soft 1px border (`rgba(140, 56, 62, 0.2)`).
- On focus: 1.5px solid terracotta wine ring (`#8C383E`) with a delicate warm glow. Floating labels use `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold in wine.

### Cards
- Surface background in `#FDF9F3` with soft 1px borders in `#EADDCB` and an ambient tinted shadow. 
- Padding defaults to `space-lg` (24px). Headers are set in `Epilogue` with body metadata in muted taupe-wine (`#5E3D3F`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square (`0.25rem` radius) or circle controls. In inactive state: 1.5px border of `#8C383E`. In selected state: solid `#8C383E` fill with an ivory tick or pip (`#F4E8D7`).

### List Items & Dividers
- Separators use soft, tinted lines (`rgba(140, 56, 62, 0.08)`).
- Hover states across list elements apply an ultra-subtle oat-tint overlay with a smooth 150ms ease transition.
